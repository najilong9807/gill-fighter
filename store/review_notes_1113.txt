What's new in 1.1.13:
(1) Still no in-app purchases (gems are earned in game only; the gem products in App Store Connect are unsubmitted drafts). No new accounts, data collection or SDKs.
(2) Deep Expedition now continues past floor 30 to floor 99 ("Endless Abyss": Sea Cave, Shipwreck Sea, Glowing Abyss and Volcanic Vent floors), with bonus gems for first clears of floors 40, 50, 70 and 99. A new in-game hazard: a volcanic eruption alert in Solo and on Volcanic Vent floors, after which embers fly for a few seconds and the player dodges them.
(3) Arena (online shared sea) is fairer and smoother: same movement speed on slow phones, smoothing for unstable connections, and much bigger rivals are drawn smaller with a red ring showing their reach. Deep Survival rankings continue after all chapters are cleared.
(4) Optional local notification for the next-day login reward. After the player has finished at least two runs, the game asks once whether to enable reminders; only if the player taps yes does the standard iOS notification permission prompt appear. Reminders can be turned off in Settings and are never sent between midnight and 8 AM.
(5) Seasonal Halloween theme continues until Nov 21 (from 1.1.11). Bug fixes, small-screen text fixes and English/Japanese text review. Ads are unchanged.

Sign-in and Game Center: there is no required account or login. Optional features: Game Center leaderboards (uses the player's existing Game Center sign-in), an optional backup code (Settings > Backup; random 12-character code, the save is stored under a hash of the code) and an optional "Apple account link" in Settings > Backup that uses Sign in with Apple only to back up and restore the same save file on another device. No name or email is stored.

Purpose and audience: Gill Fighter is a casual "eat and grow" fish game. The player guides a small fish, eats smaller fish to grow and avoids bigger ones, collects 89 fish species, levels them up with in-game pearls and decorates a personal aquarium. It is for casual mobile gamers (12+) who want short 2-5 minute sessions with one-finger controls, and Solo can be played offline.

How to use: No account, login or sample file is needed. Launch the app and tap Solo on the home screen, then choose Score Challenge or Deep Expedition. Touch and drag anywhere to move; the bottom-right button uses the special ability. Bottom tabs: Play, Tank (aquarium decoration and the aquarium house), Dex (fish collection), Shop (upgrades, decorations and boosters for in-game currency only). "Arena" is an online shared sea with other players and computer-controlled fish; there is no chat. "Friends" lets players who type the same 4-digit number share a separate Arena sea. The gear icon (top right) opens settings and an optional contact form. Progress is saved on the device; the leaderboard (random device ID, optional nickname, best score) is stored on Supabase.

External services: Google AdMob (optional rewarded video ads, non-personalized only - the player may choose to watch an ad to continue a run once), Web3Forms (delivers optional contact form messages to the developer's email), Supabase (Realtime relays Arena positions and the online player count; the database keeps leaderboard rows, optional backups and anonymous crash records) and Apple (optional Game Center and Sign in with Apple, described above). No payments, in-app purchases, AI services or analytics SDK.
