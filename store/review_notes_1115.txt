What's new in 1.1.15:
(1) No new purchases, accounts, data collection or SDKs.
(2) On first launch, new players choose a nickname (random suggestion, max 10 characters, offensive words blocked, editable in Settings). Players who skip the tutorial see three short control tips.
(3) If frames are consistently slow on a device, the game lowers the canvas resolution for the rest of that run. Very slow runs send an anonymous performance record (random device ID only) to the existing diagnostics table.
(4) Cosmetic placement fixes for rays and mantas.
(5) Weekly event (date-based, no server): each week Score Challenge gets one of three rule changes (1.5x pearls, bigger fish, more small fish). Playing 3 runs of 20+ seconds that week unlocks a reward of in-game currency (200 pearls + 3 gems). Leaderboard rows for ranks 1-3 show medals and titles. A new winter background track plays during the existing winter season.
(6) Solo runs start with a short protective bubble (4 seconds for the first 6 runs, then 2.5 seconds) so new players are not eaten instantly.
(7) New Solo pickups (never in Arena, Friends or Survival; only one on screen at a time): a clock that freezes other fish for 3 seconds, a whirlpool that pulls in nearby small fish for 3 seconds, and an occasional golden fish that swims away and gives extra in-game pearls if caught within 14 seconds. Seagull dives now only happen in the top two depth zones.
(8) Visual clean-up: notices are shown as a small card near the top, the home screen shows one info bar at a time, the shop is grouped into labelled sections, and the daily launch animation was removed.

Sign-in and Game Center: there is no required account or login. Optional features: Game Center leaderboards (uses the player's existing Game Center sign-in), an optional backup code (Settings > Backup; random 12-character code, the save is stored under a hash of the code) and an optional "Apple account link" in Settings > Backup that uses Sign in with Apple only to back up and restore the same save file on another device. No name or email is stored.

Purpose and audience: Gill Fighter is a casual "eat and grow" fish game. The player guides a small fish, eats smaller fish to grow and avoids bigger ones, collects 89 fish species, levels them up with in-game pearls and decorates a personal aquarium. It is for casual mobile gamers (12+) who want short 2-5 minute sessions with one-finger controls, and Solo can be played offline.

How to use: No account, login or sample file is needed. Launch the app and tap Solo on the home screen, then choose Score Challenge or Deep Expedition. Touch and drag anywhere to move; the bottom-right button uses the special ability. Bottom tabs: Play, Tank (aquarium decoration and the aquarium house), Dex (fish collection), Shop (upgrades, decorations and boosters for in-game currency only). "Arena" is an online shared sea with other players and computer-controlled fish; there is no chat. "Friends" lets players who type the same 4-digit number share a separate Arena sea. The gear icon (top right) opens settings and an optional contact form. Progress is saved on the device; the leaderboard (random device ID, optional nickname, best score) is stored on Supabase.

External services: Google AdMob (optional rewarded video ads, non-personalized only - the player may choose to watch an ad to continue a run once), Web3Forms (delivers optional contact form messages to the developer's email), Supabase (Realtime relays Arena positions and the online player count; the database keeps leaderboard rows, optional backups and anonymous crash and first-session play records (random device ID only)) and Apple (optional Game Center and Sign in with Apple, described above). No payments, in-app purchases, AI services or analytics SDK.
