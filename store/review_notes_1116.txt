What's new in 1.1.16:
(1) No new purchases, accounts, permissions or SDKs. New: a 2-letter country code from the device region setting (no location permission) is stored with the random device ID and shown as a flag by the nickname in the leaderboard; Settings > General can turn it off (clears it).
(2) Settings: the existing optional notification switch (local reminders only) moved to its own section.
(3) Leaderboard: new playful titles for ranks 1-3.
(4) Home: a new bell button lists the last 30 on-screen notices, stored only on the device.
(5) Settings are grouped into five tabs; no settings were added or removed.
(6) Easier start in Solo: no bigger fish spawn in the first 6 seconds; when eaten, the result screen names the fish.
(7) New players start the practice sea right away; the nickname screen appears once on Home after the first run.
(8) The result screen shows at most two short info lines.
(9) Dex > Sets & achievements: only the current tier is shown and claimed items are folded. Rewards are unchanged.
(10) Deep Survival mode: non-boss enemies are 25% larger; chained kills give stronger haptics, a brief hit-stop and a "xN" popup; awakening a weapon flashes the screen and clears nearby enemies; two awakened weapons add a combo attack every 8 seconds; a small panel shows awakening progress. Chapters 8 -> 20 (new rule per chapter), supply crates, timed event waves, and gear grades 4 -> 6 with unique Legendary effects (in-game items only).
(11) Style: 68 older look-alike items are no longer sold (owners keep them). New items are bought only with in-game gems or pearls (no real money). New "Pet" slot: a small companion follows the player's fish. Two new aquarium decorations.
(12) The in-app "What's new" popup describes these changes.

Sign-in and Game Center: there is no required account or login. Optional features: Game Center leaderboards (uses the player's existing Game Center sign-in), an optional backup code (Settings > Data; random 12-character code, the save is stored under a hash of the code) and an optional "Apple account link" in Settings > Data that uses Sign in with Apple only to back up and restore the same save file on another device. No name or email is stored.

Purpose and audience: Gill Fighter is a casual "eat and grow" fish game. The player guides a small fish, eats smaller fish to grow and avoids bigger ones, collects 89 fish species, levels them up with in-game pearls and decorates a personal aquarium. It is for casual mobile gamers (12+) who want short 2-5 minute sessions with one-finger controls, and Solo can be played offline.

How to use: No account, login or sample file is needed. Launch the app and tap Solo on the home screen, then choose Score Challenge or Deep Expedition. Touch and drag anywhere to move; the bottom-right button uses the special ability. Bottom tabs: Play, Tank (aquarium decoration and the aquarium house), Dex (fish collection), Shop (upgrades, decorations and boosters for in-game currency only). "Arena" is an online shared sea with other players and computer-controlled fish; there is no chat. "Friends" lets players who type the same 4-digit number share a separate Arena sea. The gear icon (top right) opens settings and an optional contact form. Progress is saved on the device; the leaderboard (random device ID, optional nickname, best score, optional country code) is stored on Supabase.

External services: Google AdMob (optional rewarded video ads, non-personalized only - the player may choose to watch an ad to continue a run once), Web3Forms (delivers optional contact form messages to the developer's email), Supabase (Realtime relays Arena positions and the online player count; the database keeps leaderboard rows, optional backups and anonymous crash and first-session play records (random device ID only)) and Apple (optional Game Center and Sign in with Apple, described above). No payments, in-app purchases, AI services or analytics SDK.
