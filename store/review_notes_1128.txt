What's new in 1.1.28:
(1) No new purchases, accounts, permissions or SDKs.
(2) Seasonal theme: the Halloween season ends and a Christmas season runs until December 30 (decided by the device date). It changes colors and decorations, adds a Christmas event window (7 daily gifts, 3 daily missions, collectible gift boxes that unlock in-game pearls, gems and a cosmetic crown), floating gift boxes, a Santa sleigh that passes by and drops gift boxes, and a boss fish in Solo; after the 7 daily gifts there is a small daily gift until December 25. Players also get a free decoratable Christmas tree in their aquarium and earn ornaments to hang on it, plus an event-only snowman pet. All rewards are in-game items only; nothing is sold. Players who turned on reminders in Settings may get one local notification at 7 PM about the daily gift (same opt-in notification setting as before). Halloween items players already own stay with them.
(3) Layout fix in the ranking list (long results no longer cut off names).

Sign-in and Game Center: there is no required account or login. Optional features: Game Center leaderboards (uses the player's existing Game Center sign-in), an optional backup code (Settings > Data; random 12-character code, the save is stored under a hash of the code) and an optional "Apple account link" in Settings > Data that uses Sign in with Apple only to back up and restore the same save file on another device. No name or email is stored.

Purpose and audience: Gill Fighter is a casual "eat and grow" fish game. The player guides a small fish, eats smaller fish to grow and avoids bigger ones, collects 105 fish species, levels them up with in-game pearls and decorates a personal aquarium. It is for casual mobile gamers (12+) who want short 2-5 minute sessions with one-finger controls, and Solo can be played offline.

How to use: No account, login or sample file is needed. Launch the app and tap Solo on the home screen, then choose Score Challenge or Deep Expedition. Touch and drag anywhere to move; the bottom-right button uses the special ability. Bottom tabs: Play, Tank (aquarium decoration and the aquarium house), Dex (fish collection), Shop (upgrades, decorations and boosters for in-game currency only). "Arena" is an online shared sea with other players and computer-controlled fish; there is no chat. "Friends" lets players who type the same 4-digit number share a separate Arena sea. The gear icon (top right) opens settings and an optional contact form. Progress is saved on the device; the leaderboard (random device ID, optional nickname, best score, optional country code) is stored on Supabase.

External services: Google AdMob (optional rewarded video ads, non-personalized only - the player may choose to watch an ad to continue a run once or to double the pearls from a run), Web3Forms (delivers optional contact form messages to the developer's email), Supabase (Realtime relays Arena positions and the online player count; the database keeps leaderboard rows, optional backups and anonymous crash and first-session play records (random device ID only)) and Apple (optional Game Center and Sign in with Apple, described above). No payments, in-app purchases, AI services or analytics SDK.
