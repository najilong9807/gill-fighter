What's new in 1.1.30:
(1) No new purchases, accounts, permissions or SDKs.
(2) New bottom tab "Town": a casual town-building mode (Gill City) inside the same app. The player places roads, zones and buildings, collects produced goods and delivers residents' orders. It uses the same in-game pearls and gems as the rest of the game (no real money), is saved on the device like the rest of the save, and works offline. It opens directly from the bottom tab bar; no login is needed.
(3) Aquarium: press and hold a decoration (or the coral next to the house) to pick it up and move it; after dropping it, a small "Put away" button stores it in the inventory.
(4) Aquarium "Fish friends": tapping a fish in the list opens a short story about that fish; one chapter opens per friendship heart. Once the Town's Coral Hall reaches level 2, a delivery turtle sometimes swims across the aquarium; tapping it gives a few in-game pearls (up to 3 times a day).
(5) Christmas tree: ornaments can be dragged by touch onto the tree reliably, press-and-hold also picks one up, and the ornament shop (in-game pearls only) is shown below the tree again.
The Christmas season content is unchanged and still runs until December 30.

Sign-in and Game Center: there is no required account or login. Optional features: Game Center leaderboards (uses the player's existing Game Center sign-in), an optional backup code (Settings > Data; random 12-character code, the save is stored under a hash of the code) and an optional "Apple account link" in Settings > Data that uses Sign in with Apple only to back up and restore the same save file on another device. No name or email is stored.

Purpose and audience: Gill Fighter is a casual "eat and grow" fish game. The player guides a small fish, eats smaller fish to grow and avoids bigger ones, collects 105 fish species, levels them up with in-game pearls and decorates a personal aquarium. It is for casual mobile gamers (12+) who want short 2-5 minute sessions with one-finger controls, and Solo can be played offline.

How to use: No account, login or sample file is needed. Launch the app and tap Solo on the home screen, then choose Score Challenge or Deep Expedition. Touch and drag anywhere to move; the bottom-right button uses the special ability. Bottom tabs: Play, Tank (aquarium decoration and the aquarium house), Town (Gill City town-building), Dex (fish collection), Shop (upgrades, decorations and boosters for in-game currency only). "Arena" is an online shared sea with other players and computer-controlled fish; there is no chat. "Friends" lets players who type the same 4-digit number share a separate Arena sea. The gear icon (top right) opens settings and an optional contact form. Progress is saved on the device; the leaderboard (random device ID, optional nickname, best score, optional country code) is stored on Supabase.

External services: Google AdMob (optional rewarded video ads, non-personalized only - the player may choose to watch an ad to continue a run once or to double the pearls from a run), Web3Forms (delivers optional contact form messages to the developer's email), Supabase (Realtime relays Arena positions and the online player count; the database keeps leaderboard rows, optional backups and anonymous crash and first-session play records (random device ID only)) and Apple (optional Game Center and Sign in with Apple, described above). No payments, in-app purchases, AI services or analytics SDK.
